This site is the public face of MediaTuna. Flags live in the [docs](/docs/).

What it does today:

- Batch-convert a home archive to MP4 and MP3
- Keep dates, tags, and already-finished work
- Preview with `--dry-run` and `--sample` before a full run
- Recycle Bin for deletes; SHA-256 before recup cleanup

Clone it, `pnpm install`, `npm link`. See [/install](/install).
